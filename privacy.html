PRIVACY POLICY

YOUR PRIVACY
MATTERS.

Here's how we collect, use and protect
your information at Elevate.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Elevate collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information. We aim to keep your information safe and be transparent about how it is used.

2. Information We Collect

When you join Elevate, we may collect your name, email address and membership or payment-related information.

We may also collect information you choose to share within the community, such as messages, posts or profile details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide access to Elevate, manage your membership, respond to enquiries, communicate important account information and maintain or improve the community and website.

4. How We Protect Your Information

We use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards and trusted service providers to help protect personal information. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing Your Information

We may share necessary information with service providers that help operate Elevate, such as payment processors, website hosting services and email providers.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

6. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to object to certain uses.

You can contact us to make a request. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, please contact us through our Contact page.
