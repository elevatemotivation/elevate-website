FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GOT A QUESTION?

Everything you need to know about Elevate, membership, payments, what's included and more.

What is Elevate?
Elevate is a membership community focused on helping you build income, improve your mindset and become a better version of yourself. Members get access to business ideas, step-by-step guidance, resources, a private community and weekly meetings for £4.99 per month.
Who is Elevate for?
Elevate is for people who want to improve themselves, explore business opportunities and surround themselves with others who are serious about making progress.
Do I need experience to join?
No. Elevate is designed to be approachable for beginners, while also offering useful ideas and discussions for people who have already started building something.
What do I get with my membership?
You'll get access to business models and opportunities, practical guides, content creation information, personal development resources, the Elevate community and weekly meetings.
How do I join Elevate?
Visit our Price page, choose to join and follow the membership sign-up instructions.
Is there a contract?
No long-term commitment. Elevate is a monthly membership that you can cancel whenever you choose.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time. See our Terms and Refund Policy for more information.
How much does Elevate cost?
Elevate costs £4.99 per month, equivalent to approximately £1.15 per week.
What payment methods do you accept?
Available payment methods will be displayed securely during checkout.
Can I get a refund?
Please read our Refund Policy for information about eligibility and how to contact us.
What kind of business models do you cover?
We explore low-cost business ideas, TikTok Shop, content creation, service-based businesses and other online and offline opportunities.
Will more content be added?
Yes. We aim to keep expanding Elevate with new resources, ideas and practical guidance as the community grows.
How do the weekly meetings work?
Weekly group meetings are designed for questions, business discussions, sharing progress and learning alongside other Elevate members.
How is Elevate different from free groups?
Elevate is built around practical learning, ongoing resources, structured support and a community that encourages members to take action.
Will joining guarantee that I make money?
No. Elevate provides information, ideas and community support, but your results depend on your circumstances, decisions and effort. Earnings are never guaranteed.
Still have a question?
We're happy to help. Visit our Contact page and send us your question.
